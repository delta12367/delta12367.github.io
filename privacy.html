Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

myapp12 ("the app") is a personal tool that publishes videos to its owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

Information the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to your channel and to post a comment on those videos. It requests the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl permissions.
  • TikTok: with your permission, the app uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to read your basic profile info and publish videos to your account.

How information is used

Access is used only to publish videos, captions and comments to your own accounts. The app does not read your private videos, messages, subscribers or analytics, and it never shows ads.

Storage

Login tokens are stored only on the machine or private server that runs the app. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than Google or TikTok when making the API calls described above.

Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the app with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions page or from TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's token files also removes its access.
